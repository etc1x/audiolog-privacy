Audiolog privacy policy

Applies to the Audiolog audiobook player for Android (app ID com.audiobooklog.app). Last updated 23 October 2026.

In short

Audiolog does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your audiobooks, listening history, stats, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your library (book titles, authors, covers, chapters), your place in each book, bookmarks and their notes, resume points, listening history, stats, streaks and badges, and your settings are stored only in Audiolog's own storage on your phone.
  • Audiolog only reads the folders you choose. It never changes or deletes your audio files.
  • Progress files (optional, on by default): to keep your place safe, Audiolog can save a small hidden text file next to each book in the folders you chose. It contains that book's place, bookmarks and edits, and nothing else. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Backups: when you export a backup, Audiolog writes a text file to the place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards (for example, copying it to a cloud drive) is up to you.

When Audiolog uses the internet

Audiolog works fully offline. It only goes online when you ask it to:

  • Finding a cover or ISBN (Book info, Find online): the search words, usually the book's title and author, are sent to Open Library (Internet Archive) and to Apple's public iTunes Search service, which return matching covers. No other information is sent. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.
  • Links you open, such as Goodreads, StoryGraph or a web image search, open in your browser or in that app, and that service's privacy policy applies.
  • Chromecast (Google Play version only): when you cast, Audiolog uses Google's Cast library and streams the current book to your Chromecast over your own Wi-Fi network, only while casting.
  • Purchases and tips (Google Play version): payments are handled entirely by Google Play. Audiolog never sees your payment details; it only learns whether Pro was bought.

Watch app (optional)

If you turn on "Allow the watch app to connect" in Settings, Audiolog runs a small server on your phone so the Audiolog Remote watch app can show what's playing and control playback. It is off by default, only accepts requests with a secret code you copy into the watch app, and only answers on your phone itself (through the Zepp app). No data leaves your phone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while a book is playing.
  • Background playback (foreground service and keeping the phone awake): so a book keeps playing with the screen off.
  • Internet: only for the features listed above.
  • Access to your audiobook folders is granted by you through Android's folder picker and can be removed at any time in Settings.

Children

Audiolog is a general audiobook player and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
